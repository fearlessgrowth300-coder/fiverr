How are my key and data handled?

This service processes your Apify token to verify your account and run research you request. It encrypts the token into a workspace key valid for 90 days. That workspace key grants access to your connected research; keep it private. Your chat provider will receive it when you use the tools.

Job metadata and collected research are saved in a named fiverr-gig-studio store in your Apify account. We do not put your raw API token in that store or browser storage. Revoke the token in Apify to stop research access. Clear this page to remove the displayed workspace key.
